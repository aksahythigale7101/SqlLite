1. Primary Key
2. Foreign Key

3. Unique Key ex-->Email VARCHAR(100) UNIQUE
4. Composite Key-->Two or more columns together uniquely identify a row.
                example-->(PRIMARY KEY (StudentId, CourseId))
5. Candidate Key -->Understand which columns could potentially be a Primary Key.
                example-->EmployeeId → unique
                          Email      → unique
                          Phone      → unique
                 All could potentially identify an employee, so they are candidate keys. One is selected as the Primary Key.
6. Alternate Key-->Candidate key that was not selected as the Primary Key.
               example -->EmployeeId → Primary Key
                          Email      → Alternate Key
                          Phone      → Alternate Key
